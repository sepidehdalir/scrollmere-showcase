How to join screenshots

Import → reorder → inspect each join → adjust overlap or select no overlap → accept → preview → export. Tap a source to trim repeated headers or footers. Suggestions can be wrong; inspect the full output before sharing.

Free and Lifetime Pro

Free includes up to three screenshots per document and PNG export. Pro adds up to 20 and PDF. Restore purchases from the document menu or upgrade sheet using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Supported sizes

JPEG, PNG and natively decodable HEIC/HEIF. Each source: up to 20 MP, 60 MB and 32,760 pixels per edge. Import up to 120 MB per batch. PNG output: up to 32 MP and 32,760 pixels per edge. PDF is paginated A4 raster content without OCR. Mixed widths remain at native size, centered on white.

Recovery and privacy

Your current draft saves locally. Original imports remain until you explicitly delete unused files. Drafts are excluded from backups; save an export if you need a durable copy elsewhere. If an export exceeds limits, try fewer images or a PDF. The app never silently lowers resolution to report success.
