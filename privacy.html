Data we collect

Scrollmere does not collect your screenshots, filenames, document contents, usage events, identifiers or location. It has no account, tracking, advertising SDK, remote analytics provider or screenshot-processing server.

Local processing and storage

You select images through Photos or Files. Image analysis, edits, previews and exports run on your device. The app retains imported originals and the current draft in its private storage, excluded from backups. Uninstalling removes these local files. Starting a new document does not automatically delete imported originals; use Delete unused local originals to remove retained copies. Your original Photos/Files items are never deleted by Scrollmere.

Exports

Content leaves the app only through an export or share you initiate. Your chosen destination may use cloud storage or its own privacy policy. Temporary exports are removed after sharing completes; abandoned exports are cleaned on a later launch after 24 hours.

Purchases and reviews

Apple handles payment and purchase restoration. Scrollmere checks verified StoreKit entitlements. Non-sensitive successful-share counts and review-request dates are stored locally. These are not transmitted to us. Apple may provide aggregate store or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings.

Support and updates

If you email support, we receive what you voluntarily include and use it to respond. Please avoid sending private screenshots unless necessary. Contact hello@arvinify.com for privacy questions. Material policy changes will be reflected here.
